This is the warm-up. It exists because the rest of the term assumes you can get data into Python and trust what comes out.

Use AI for all of it. Every line of code here is something Gemini or ChatGPT will write for you in seconds, and you should let it. What the AI will not do is notice when the answer is wrong — and this assignment is mostly a tour of ways a notebook returns a confident, plausible, incorrect number.
